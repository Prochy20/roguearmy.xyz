# Colors

The RGA palette is built for dark interfaces. Three brand colors — Green, Cyan, and Magenta — are high-saturation neon accents designed to pop against near-black backgrounds.

The values below come directly from `src/lib/design-tokens.ts`. Both this page and the `/brand` route on the live site read from the same source.

## Brand colors

These are the core identity colors:

- **Green** — primary accent for CTAs, active states, and the logo glow.
- **Cyan** — secondary accent for links, highlights, and technical UI.
- **Magenta** — tertiary accent for warnings, special badges, and visual tension.

<ColorPalette>
  {BRAND_COLORS.map((c) => (
    <ColorItem key={c.hex} title={c.name} subtitle={c.tailwind} colors={{ [c.name]: c.hex }} />
  ))}
</ColorPalette>

> **Never** use brand colors as large background fills — they are accent colors only. On dark backgrounds, use them for text, borders, glows, and small UI elements. If you need a colored background area, use the color at 5-10% opacity (e.g. `bg-rga-green/5`).

## Background colors

Four levels of darkness create depth and hierarchy. Layer them from dark to light: **Void → Primary → Elevated → Surface**. Never skip levels (e.g. don't place Surface directly on Void).

## Text colors

Three text hierarchy levels:

- **Primary** (#FFFFFF) — headings and important content.
- **Secondary** (#888888) — body text and descriptions.
- **Muted** (#555555) — labels, timestamps, placeholders, disabled text.

<ColorPalette>
  {TEXT_COLORS.map((c) => (
    <ColorItem key={c.hex} title={c.name} subtitle={c.tailwind} colors={{ [c.name]: c.hex }} />
  ))}
</ColorPalette>

> Never use brand colors (green, cyan, magenta) for long-form body text — they are for short labels, links, and accents only.

## Glow colors

Semi-transparent versions of our brand colors, used exclusively for `box-shadow` and glow effects. These create the signature neon luminosity that defines our cyberpunk aesthetic.
